READ BEFORE QUESTING

Adventurer’s field guide

An incident tracker with a very questionable naming convention.

1. Sound the alarm

Record what broke, the affected service, and who’s investigating. Choose the severity based on impact.

SEV-1 · Critical — The realm is down. Drop everything.

SEV-2 · Major — Something is degraded. Gather the party.

SEV-3 · Minor — A smaller issue. Still worth a quest.

2. Leave a trail

Open an incident to add notes. Use Investigating while finding the cause and Monitoring while verifying a fix.

3. Restore the peace

Choose Resolved and document the fix. Your victory joins the hall. If trouble returns, reopen it with an investigation note.

Example incidents are fictional. Changes are stored in a real SQLite database on the server. No spells, AI calls, or actual cloud remediation happen here.
